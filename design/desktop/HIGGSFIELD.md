# Prompts for Higgsfield: the desktop Play screen

Two kinds of picture: full mockups of the screen (to compare ideas), and wide scenes (the art a full-screen
desktop layout needs; today's six scenes are portrait, 768×1376, and blur when stretched across a monitor).
Attach `img/world/pond.webp` and `img/frog-plain/ponder.webp` as references so the style matches.

## Mockups (16:9, image model)

**A, the pond is the screen**
> Desktop game UI mockup, 16:9, cozy pixel-art style with chunky pixels, flat bright colours, dark navy outlines.
> The whole screen is a pixel-art pond seen from the bank: teal water, lily pads, pink water lilies, tall reeds at the edges.
> A cute green cartoon frog with round glasses sits on a lily pad in the centre, pencil and notepad, thinking.
> Top centre: a large cream speech card with navy border reading "How many kilometres long is one light-year?" in a bold pixel font.
> Docked at the bottom, full width: a dark navy control bar with a cream logarithmic ruler (labels 1B, 10B, 1T, 10T) and a green highlighted range,
> five cream number buttons (10, 25, 50, 70, 100, one highlighted yellow) and a big pink "Catch it" button. Clean, very readable, no clutter.

**B, three columns**
> Desktop game UI mockup, 16:9, pixel-art game. Left column: a pixel logo "Fermi Frog", a large cream question card, a ruler slider,
> five number buttons and a big pink button. Centre: a tall portrait window showing a pixel-art pond at sunset with a green frog in glasses on a lily pad and a fly.
> Right column on dark navy: a vertical progress meter with six round badges (pond, reeds, trunk, canopy, clouds, moon) and a list of five rounds with scores.
> Flat colours: navy #1B2340, cream #F7F3E3, yellow #FFD23F, pink #F0648C. Readable large type.

**C, the horizon**
> Desktop game UI mockup, 16:9, pixel art. Top 60%: a wide pixel-art pond scene with reeds, a green frog in glasses on a lily pad sitting right on the horizon line,
> a fly hovering. Big cream pixel-font question with navy outline across the sky. Bottom 40%: deep water fading to dark navy, holding a full-width cream ruler slider,
> five chunky number buttons and a big pink "Catch it" button, small keyboard hints underneath. Calm, polished, highly readable.

## Wide scenes (16:9, for the real game)

One per zone, same composition rules as the portrait ones: the perch area stays clear in the lower middle, nothing busy behind the top-centre question.

> Wide 16:9 pixel-art game background, chunky pixels, soft dawn light, no characters, no text. A calm pond: teal water with pixel ripples,
> lily pads and pink water lilies near the edges, tall reeds and cattails framing left and right, a big tree trunk on the far left, misty trees on the far bank.
> Keep the centre of the water open and the upper middle quiet.

Swap the subject for the other zones: *among tall reeds*, *against a huge tree trunk*, *in the green canopy*, *above the clouds*, *on the moon's grey surface with Earth in a starry sky*.
